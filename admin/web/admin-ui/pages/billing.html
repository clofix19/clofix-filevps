Billing Overview

Financial lists stay empty until a subscription, invoice or manual payment is recorded.

Live resources

Scanned VPS, CKS clusters, load balancers, databases, deployed applications, volumes, backups, S3 and EFS. CKS bills a control-plane unit plus node CPU/RAM/disk; attached LBs stay on the Load Balancer row. Amounts are the current monthly estimate from Settings → Resource Pricing. A stopped VPS or database is billed for storage only. Hourly usage is tracked on Due bill.

Due bill

Hourly Standard or Low cost usage. Deleted resources stay here until invoiced. A 5 or 10 minute run is billed for those minutes, not a full hour.

Monthly analysis

Paid invoices only. Amounts are shown in invoice currency plus USD and BDT using the snapshot FX rate.
